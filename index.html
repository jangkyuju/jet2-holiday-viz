Rising insight 2

Every route bottoms out at a different time. Every route ends in red.

The staircase breaks into 10-day stretches. Each stretch flips into a tile: blue if the fare fell, red if it rose. Then every other route from Atlanta lines up beneath it.

There is no single best day to book. But the last ten days are red on every route.

Solution

One more check

Price the nonstop before you assume the connection is cheaper.

Two planes, same trip. The length of each contrail is the median fare. In our sample, nonstop came out cheaper in every travel period.

Caveat: connecting itineraries skew toward longer routes, so part of this gap may come from route mix. The final version compares within the same route.

01 / WHEREPick where with the mapSome destinations cost far less to reach from your airport.
02 / WATCHINGDon't watch it dailyDay-to-day moves are noise with nothing to act on.

Data

Expedia Flight Prices (Kaggle, dilwong/flightprices): one-way fares from 16 U.S. airports, searched April 16 to October 5, 2022. We derived days before departure, route and holiday period. This prototype uses placeholder numbers shaped to our exploration results.

Travel periods

  • Memorial Day: flights May 26–31
  • July 4: flights Jun 30 – Jul 5
  • Labor Day: flights Sep 1–6
  • Non-holiday: Jun 9–14 and Aug 11–16

Limits

  • Memorial Day searches only reach 44 days out.
  • One-way fares only; no round trips or award tickets.
  • Seat counts of 0 are treated as missing.
  • Each check is compared with the same flight's previous check.
