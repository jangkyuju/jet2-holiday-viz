Rising insight 1

Zoom out, and fares don't creep up. They jump.

Every flight in the dataset, compared with its own price the day before: 64.5 million comparisons, not just holidays. For six weeks a typical fare barely moves. Then it jumps right after the 21-, 14- and 7-day marks, and once more the day before departure.

Rising insight 2

Every route bottoms out at a different time. Every route ends in red.

The staircase breaks into 10-day stretches. Each stretch flips into a tile: blue if the fare fell, red if it rose. Then every other route from Atlanta lines up beneath it.

There is no single best day to book. But the last ten days are red on every route.

One more check

Price the nonstop before you assume the connection is cheaper.

Two planes, same trip. The length of each contrail is the median fare. Across all routes, nonstop came out cheaper in every travel period. Within the same route it still held in 714 of 819 route-and-period comparisons.

Switch to your route to compare within one route. A few routes flip the pattern: from Atlanta to Boston, connecting was cheaper.

Data

Expedia Flight Prices (Kaggle, dilwong/flightprices): one-way fares from 16 U.S. airports, searched April 16 to October 5, 2022. Map: average fare from Atlanta (Arrington Goss). Diary and staircase: daily lowest fares and medians (Cynthia Zhou). Nonstop vs. connecting: median fares, overall and within route (Yuhan Hu). The route grid rows and the fare-band flows are still placeholders.

Travel periods

  • Memorial Day: flights May 26–31
  • July 4: flights Jun 30 – Jul 5
  • Labor Day: flights Sep 1–6
  • Non-holiday: Jun 9–14 and Aug 11–16

Limits

  • Memorial Day searches only reach 44 days out.
  • One-way fares only; no round trips or award tickets.
  • Seat counts of 0 are treated as missing.
  • Each check is compared with the same flight's previous check.
