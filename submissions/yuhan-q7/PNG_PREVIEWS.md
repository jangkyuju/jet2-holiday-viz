# Q7 static chart images

These PNGs use the same validated eight-row data as the Tableau workbook. They were generated separately, not exported from Tableau. PNGs are static; use the CSV/JSON for holiday switching on the website.

- [Memorial Day](Q7_Memorial_Day.png) — May 26–31, 2022
- [July 4](Q7_July_4.png) — June 30–July 5, 2022
- [Labor Day](Q7_Labor_Day.png) — September 1–6, 2022
- [Non-holiday](Q7_Non_holiday.png) — June 9–14 and August 11–16, 2022
- [All four periods](Q7_All_Periods.png)

All charts share a $0–$500 scale. The layover path is decorative and does not encode stop location, time, or fare.

These are pooled medians of observed Expedia quotes, not unique flights or sales. Route and booking-time mix differ, so the difference is not an adjusted nonstop premium. Details are in README.md.
